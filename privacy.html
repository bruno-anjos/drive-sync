Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Drive Sync is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

Only the files and folders in Google Drive that Drive Sync created itself (Google's drive.file scope). It can't read, list or change any other file in the Drive.

What it does with the data

It copies those files between the owner's own server and the owner's own Drive. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. There are no analytics and no tracking.

Drive Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it's stored

On the owner's server and in the owner's Google Drive. The access token stays on the owner's server.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account's third-party connections. Files already in Drive stay there until deleted.
